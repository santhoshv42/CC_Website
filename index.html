Prophetic Ministry

Prophet Michael Manju

FOUNDER OF CALVARY CHURCH - JESUS MISSION MINISTRIES (BANGALORE)

For 14 years, Prophet Michael Manju suffered from severe stammering and struggled to speak fluently. During those years, he visited many pilgrimage places, seeking a solution and hoping to experience a miracle, but nothing changed.

His father had experienced a similar condition, and other members of his family were also affected. Despite seeking help and visiting different pilgrimage places, there was no breakthrough.

Coming from an Orthodox Hindu family, Michael was introduced to Jesus Christ. He accepted Jesus and began to put his faith in Him.

Within six months, he experienced a remarkable transformation. After 14 years of severe stammering, he was able to speak fluently.

The generational curse that had affected his family was broken, and his life was completely transformed. Today, Prophet Michael Manju is able to preach the Word of God fluently in Tamil, Kannada, Telugu, and English, sharing the message of Jesus Christ with people across different communities.

His testimony stands as a powerful reminder of the transformation he experienced through his faith in Jesus Christ.
