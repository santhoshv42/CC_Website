Prophetic Ministry

Prophet Michael Manju

Founder & President of Jesus Mission Ministries (Bangalore)

Prophet Michael Manju was called into full-time ministry with a clear commission: to awaken believers to their spiritual inheritance, release the living Prophetic Word, minister divine healing, and establish vibrant worship communities under Calvary Church.

Currently based in Bangalore, the ministry actively attends, hosts, and ministers at meetings, crusade gatherings, and prayer conventions as the Lord opens doors and provides the way.

Known for an accurate prophetic unction and divine revelation, Prophet Michael Manju releases timely prophetic words, personal spiritual direction, and God’s wisdom that bring breakthrough, break bondages, and align believers with their heavenly calling.
